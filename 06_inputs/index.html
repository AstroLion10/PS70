Below is a photo of my circuit configuration: </p> <image style = "width: 50%"src = "../06_inputs/wk6CapSensorCircuit.jpg" alt = "Capacitive Sensor Circuit"> </image> <br></br> <h4>Assignment 2: Flex/Pressure Sensor</h4> <p class="margin"> I created a velostat flex sensor to use for the soft robotic glove. This sensor can help with finger motion tracking in addition to glove actuator automation for the final project. </p> <p class="margin"> To make the sensor, I followed instructions on <a href="https://lucstechblog.blogspot.com/2018/04/flex-and-pressure-sensors.html">Lucs Tech Blog</a>. First, I took a piece of masking tape. Then, I cut a short length of copper tape and gently folded the sticky side onto itself to make a flexible, thin copper plate. I centered this copper piece on the tape. After this, I cut two identical, small velostat rectangles. Then, I placed one rectangle on top of the copper plate (the velostat should be wider than the copper piece to cover the whole copper plate). I added the other piece of velostat on top of the first and placed the next copper plate (making sure it did not touch the first copper plate). Lastly, I sealed the other side with masking tape. This made the finished sensor. I would recommend using a more insulating tape material for future velostat sensors, as touching the paper masking tape with a conductive hand might potentially affect the sensor reading. Here is the finished sensor: </p> <image style = "width: 50%"src = "../06_inputs/wk6FlexSensor1.jpg" alt = "Flex Sensor"> </image> <br></br> <p class="margin"> To test it, I hooked up both of the copper ends to a multimeter and measured the sensor's resistance when it was bent at different angles. </p> <image style = "width: 50%"src = "../06_inputs/wk6FlexMultimeter.jpg" alt = "Multimeter"> </image> <image style = "width: 50%"src = "../06_inputs/wkSixSensorAngleBending.jpg" alt = "Angle Measure"> </image> <p class="margin"> Then, I connected the sensor to Arduino and graphed the approximate Arduino values returned at different bend angles. </p> <image style = "width: 50%"src = "../06_inputs/wkSixFlexCircuitforCallibration.jpg" alt = "Callibration Circuit"> </image> <p class="margin"> Below is the best-fit line I computed through Desmos in order to calibrate the sensor: </p> <image style = "width: 50%"src = "../06_inputs/wkSixArduinoCallibrationGraph.png" alt = "Callibration Graph"> </image> <p class="margin"> Using the equation of this line, I edited the Arduino code to print the approximate bend angle of the sensor. I tested the degree value using the serial plotter to make sure the code was accurate. Then, I altered the circuit to include three LEDs and I re-programmed the code to light up the blue LED if the angle was bent by 30 degrees or less, the blue and the white if between 60 and 30 degrees, and all three LEDs if greater than 60 degrees.
